Research Interests

Statistical causal inference

Under statistical constraints, I study counterfactual structures and quantify how challenges arising in applied settings affect intervention effects. I am also interested in connections between statistical causal inference and mathematical concepts such as algebra and geometry.

Statistical machine learning

I work on the theory of statistical models for data with complex structures, such as high-dimensional data and network dependence. In particular, I am interested in theoretically and quantitatively analyzing how model structures and regularization affect statistical inference and predictive uncertainty.

Deep learning theory

I study the generalization performance and learning dynamics of deep learning models mathematically. In particular, I am interested in what kinds of mathematical and physical structures these models and their learning methods can be understood to correspond to.
